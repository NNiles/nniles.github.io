01 About

Climate, institutions,
and public policy.

I bring more than a decade of applied research and university teaching experience across Sri Lanka and Australia.

I am a Lecturer (Practice) at Monash University and a PhD researcher at the University of Melbourne. My doctoral research examines climate adaptation governance involving external partners, development finance, and infrastructure safeguards.

My research and policy experience includes work with the Centre for Poverty Analysis, the International Organization for Migration, and Janathakshan. I hold a Master of Laws in Public International Law and a BSc in International Relations from the University of London.
